LandIS Assistant — privacy notice

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who we are

This assistant is part of the LandIS work at Cranfield University for the National Soil Data Portal for England and Wales. For privacy questions, contact the LandIS team at Cranfield University.

What we collect

When you use the assistant we store:

  • the text of your questions and the assistant’s replies
  • the identifiers of the passages used in an answer
  • a random session id kept in this browser tab
  • the date and time of each message

We do not create user accounts. We do not store IP addresses or browser user-agent strings. Please do not type personal data into the chat.

Why we process this data

We store questions and answers so the assistant can follow a short conversation, and so the team can check whether answers are grounded in the corpus.

Who processes your messages

Your question, and a few earlier turns when needed, are sent to Mistral AI for OCR of the corpus, embeddings, and the written answer. The application and database are hosted on Render.

How long we keep data

Chat records are kept for 12 months and then deleted. The session id in your browser ends when you close the tab or start a new chat.
